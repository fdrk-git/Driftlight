## v0.0.6 · 2026-11-28 · Pre-alpha

A farm that grows, and blends in.

### Highlights

- **A pen that grows:** the shop has a new **Bigger pen** button. Each one adds a bay of fence, to the left or down (the shorter side first), up to 6 by 6 bays, with a bigger floor and room for more animals: 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14. It costs 240 light, and 1.6 times more each time. If plots are in the way on one side, the pen grows the other way; if both are blocked, the button says there is no room.
- **Ground that blends in:** the soil round the plots no longer stops at a straight edge. It thins out into the meadow with a ragged edge, crumbs of earth and grass leaning over it. The pen floor is trampled grass and straw that fades into the meadow too.
- **Plots that connect:** plots above and below each other now sit as close together as plots side by side, so a field reads as one tilled bed. A plot's rim is dark soil instead of an outline.
- The shop's buttons are a little shorter, so one more fits on small screens.

### Good to know

- Saves from earlier versions carry over, with the small pen the game started with.
- This is still an early test version, and the numbers (what a bigger pen costs, how many animals it holds) are first guesses, so tell us how the pace feels.
- The Windows build has not been tried on a real Windows computer yet.
- The builds aren't signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn the first time you open the game. The [README](https://github.com/fdrk-git/Driftlight#download-and-play) says how to get past that.

## v0.0.5 · 2026-11-28 · Pre-alpha

A cosier farm. (v0.0.2 was a local test version and was never released.)

### Highlights

- **A fairy-light fence:** the pen is now fenced with warm logs, with flowers and moss at the posts, lanterns, and strings of fairy lights that sway in the wind and flicker. They stay bright when the world goes dark.
- **A new helper drone:** a small bee-bot with a round display face and wings that flap smoothly. It still has five moods: happy, working, blink, wow and love.
- **A cosy forest all round:** a line of tree houses and giant mushrooms behind the field, groves and clearings in the meadow, small stumps, logs, woodpiles, ferns and flowers, and distant mushroom silhouettes on the horizon. At night the windows light up, the glow comes on and fairy lights hang between the trees. A new plot clears whatever it would stand on, so the forest steps back as the farm grows.
- **Plots where you want them:** **New plot** in the shop asks where it goes, and you can place up to 24 plots anywhere on the land. Scroll, use the arrow keys or drag with the right mouse button to move the view; in the corner, small arrows at the window's edges do it too.
- **Plants sit properly:** plants now stand lower in their plots and sway and hop on whole pixels, so they stay sharp. The helper drone no longer jumps to one side when it turns around.
- **Cinderrift is out for now:** the lava planet and the Planets button are gone while the game gets its look in order. Mossrill is the only planet.

### Good to know

- Saves from earlier versions carry over: plots keep their places (old saves put them in rows of four), and the light is kept. If you had settled Cinderrift in v0.0.4, this version keeps Mossrill and your light, but it does not keep the Cinderrift farm.
- This is still an early test version, and the numbers (how fast crops grow, what things cost) are first guesses.
- The Windows build has not been tried on a real Windows computer yet.
- The builds aren't signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn the first time you open the game. The [README](https://github.com/fdrk-git/Driftlight#download-and-play) says how to get past that.

## v0.0.4 · 2026-11-26 · Pre-alpha

A second planet.

### Highlights

- **Cinderrift:** a volcanic world with a dark red sky and smoking volcanoes. Three new crops (Emberbulb, Cinderthistle, Obsidian Lotus) and two new animals (Slagback, Embermoth) pay a lot more than Mossrill's, and have their own goods and props.
- **Planets:** the full view has a **Planets** button at the top. Settle Cinderrift once for 1500 light, then click a planet to travel there. Each planet has its own field, pen and helper drone, and the light is shared.
- **All planets keep running:** the planet you left keeps growing and its drone keeps working. The corner window shows the planet you looked at last.
- **Old saves carry over:** a save from an earlier version loads as Mossrill.

### Good to know

- All the Cinderrift numbers are first guesses.
- The Windows build has not been tried on a real Windows computer yet.

## v0.0.3 · 2026-11-26 · Pre-alpha

A new look for the farm. (v0.0.2 was a local test version and was never released; its changes are included here.)

### Highlights

- **One pixel size for everything:** crops, animals, props, item icons and the drone now share the same pixel size, with a clean one-pixel outline, instead of a mix of sizes.
- **Sharp pixels on screen:** nothing in the corner view or the full view is blurred any more. Text, icons, frames, stars and sprites sit on whole pixels, and the full view is sized to a whole multiple of the pixel size.
- **A new helper drone:** rebuilt from clean parts (framed solar panel, rotor with yellow tips, pulsing antenna ball, a small jet underneath) with five moods: it blinks now and then, squints while it works, looks wowed when you upgrade it, and shows hearts after it finishes a job.

### Good to know

- This is still an early test version. The numbers (growth times, prices) are first guesses.
- The Windows build has not been tried on a real Windows computer yet.

## v0.0.1 · 2026-11-25 · Pre-alpha

The first version of Driftlight. It is a small farm that sits in the corner of your screen and plays itself while you work.

### Highlights

- **The farm on Mossrill:** three alien crops (Glowcap, Spiralvine, Prismfern) grow through five stages, and two animals (Puffball, Mossback) wander their pen and make goods. Everything is drawn in pixel art and animated: crops sway and sparkle when ripe, animals walk and hover, and the world drifts from day into night.
- **A helper drone does the chores:** it harvests, replants and collects, one job at a time, for about 70% of what a click earns. Click a ripe crop or a bubble over an animal to do it yourself, at once, for the full value.
- **Two views of one farm:** the corner view is for a glance and a quick harvest. The open button grows the farm to the whole screen with a shop for plots, seeds, animals and a better drone. **Esc** goes back.
- **It keeps going while you are away:** the farm runs for up to 8 hours while the game is closed, and a "Welcome back" panel says what it brought.

### Good to know

- This is an early test version. The numbers (growth times, prices) are first guesses.
- The Windows build has not been tried on a real Windows computer before this release.
