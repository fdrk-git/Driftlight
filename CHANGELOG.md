## v0.1.1 · 2026-11-17 · Alpha

Two more ways to lend a hand. As always, both are optional: the game keeps going without you.

### Highlights

- **Pulse:** click the station and every probe flies twice as fast for 10 seconds. A ring of dots around the station shows it: gold while it runs, filling up while it recharges for a minute, and fully lit when it's ready again.
- **Finds:** every few minutes a scan turns up a wreck or an anomaly, and a small panel offers two rewards. Take its light now, or get double light from every scan for a minute. A bar shows the time left to pick; after 20 seconds the game takes the light for you.

### Changes

- While double light lasts, a gold counter under your light shows the seconds left.
- Two new sounds, one for a Pulse and one for a find.

## v0.1.0 · 2026-11-17 · Alpha

The first playable version. Driftlight is a small square window that stays on top in the corner of your screen and plays itself while you work.

### Highlights

- Probes leave the station on their own, scan a planet and carry its light home. Every scanned planet is replaced by a new find: sixteen kinds in three sizes, some with rings, some with land and seas.
- Three upgrades to spend light on: Fleet launches one more probe (up to six), Engine makes probes faster, and Scanner brings more light from every scan.
- You can join in: click a planet and the next free probe goes there first and scans it twice as fast, or click a passing comet to catch a burst of light.

### Also in this version

- Pixel art for the planets, the station, the probes and the comet, with a matching interface and two pixel fonts.
- Four quiet sound effects: light coming home, marking a planet, buying an upgrade and catching a comet.
- A menu behind the three bars, with Settings (window size in three steps, and volume), a quick Mute, and Quit.
- The game saves on its own every ten seconds and when you quit.
- Drag any empty spot to move the window.
