## v0.0.1 · 2026-11-25 · Pre-alpha

The first version of Driftlight. It is a small farm that sits in the corner of your screen and plays itself while you work.

### Highlights

- **The farm on Mossrill:** three alien crops (Glowcap, Spiralvine, Prismfern) grow through five stages, and two animals (Puffball, Mossback) wander their pen and make goods. Everything is drawn in pixel art and animated: crops sway and sparkle when ripe, animals walk and hover, and the world drifts from day into night.
- **A helper drone does the chores:** it harvests, replants and collects, one job at a time, for about 70% of what a click earns. Click a ripe crop or a bubble over an animal to do it yourself, at once, for the full value.
- **Two views of one farm:** the corner view is for a glance and a quick harvest. The open button grows the farm to the whole screen with a shop for plots, seeds, animals and a better drone. **Esc** goes back.
- **It keeps going while you are away:** the farm runs for up to 8 hours while the game is closed, and a "Welcome back" panel says what it brought.

### Good to know

- This is an early test version. The numbers (growth times, prices) are first guesses.
- The Windows build has not been tried on a real Windows computer before this release.
